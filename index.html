Who I Am

A programmer who builds things and isn't afraid to figure it out.

I'm a Computer Science student with a growing collection of projects and a genuine hunger to learn. Programming isn't just something I study — it's something I do. I've shipped real projects, worked through real problems, and picked up a range of skills along the way.

What sets me apart isn't a list of credentials — it's the mindset. I'm the person who dives into something new without waiting to be told how. I thrive under pressure, stay sharp when things get hectic, and actually enjoy the challenge of figuring things out on the fly.

I work well in a team and I'm comfortable leading one when needed. Whether I'm writing code, designing a workflow, or coordinating with people, I show up ready to contribute and take ownership. I believe good work comes from staying curious, communicating clearly, and never being too proud to learn something new.
